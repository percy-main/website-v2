Percy Main Community Sports Club is home to BKFC Gym, offering amateur boxing training and development for the local community. Whether you're looking to get fit, learn self-defence, or train competitively, the gym provides coaching for all ages and abilities in a supportive environment.

Boxing at Percy Main is about more than just the ring. The gym focuses on building confidence, discipline, and fitness while fostering a strong sense of community. Sessions cover technique, pad work, conditioning, and sparring for those who want to take it further.
