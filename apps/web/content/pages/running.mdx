Percy Main Runners welcome anyone into the fold who runs, wants to run, or even thinks about wanting to run!

Formed in 2025, our core values are:

- Inclusion
- Fun
- Improvement

### Regular Events

You'll currently find members running at _Whitley Bay Park Run_ and _Rising Sun Park Run_ every Saturday - look out for their [Percy Main club gear](https://kit.percymain.org).

We're looking to host our own events, training, and competitions soon! In the meantime, why not join our park run club and represent The Main?
