Percy Main Amateurs FC represent the club in grassroots football, competing in the **Northern Football Alliance League**. Based at Purvis Park in the heart of Percy Main, the team holds England Football Accreditation and FA Respect certification, reflecting our commitment to fair play and development at every level.

The men's first team plays competitive fixtures throughout the season under the governance of Northumberland FA. Whether you're an experienced player or looking to get back into the game, Percy Main Amateurs FC is a welcoming place to play regular, organised football in the local community.

### Get Involved

For fixtures, results, and squad information, visit the team on [Pitchero](https://www.pitchero.com/clubs/percymainamateursfc/). If you're interested in joining or just want to find out more, get in touch on 07960 189667 or come down to Purvis Park, St Johns Green, Percy Main, NE29 6HS.
