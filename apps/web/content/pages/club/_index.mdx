# Welcome to Percy Main Community Sports Club

Percy Main Community Sports Club is a vibrant community sports club based in North Shields, Tyne and Wear. Founded in 1864, we are one of the oldest cricket clubs in the North East.

## What We Offer

We offer cricket for all ages and abilities, from junior coaching through to senior competitive cricket in the North Tyneside Cricket League.

## Get Involved

Whether you're a seasoned cricketer or completely new to the game, we'd love to welcome you to Percy Main.
