Sponsoring a local sports team is a great way to support your local community, and promote your business. All businesses – whether local or national – want to show they are part of the community, and sport can be the most efficient way of demonstrating this. Support us, and we will support you!

Percy Main Cricket and Sports Club offers a wide range of different sponsorship options, so you can choose what's right for you and your business. We maintain a strong presence across various social media platforms, including  and Instagram, as well as this website, and the ECB's play-cricket platform. Our sponsors benefit from a coordinated schedule of posts across all of these outlets.

#### Sponsorship Opportunities

##### Match Ball Sponsorship - £50

- Sponsor any 1XI or 2XI match of your choosing
- Sponsorship announcement post across all our social media
- Included in all public announcements for the game week, e.g. team announcements, results, videos, as well as plugs for your business
- Drink and tea for two at the sponsored game

##### Player Sponsorship - £100

- Sponsor any senior player
- Sponsorship announcement post across all our social media
- Appear next to that player on all team sheets, and any results where they are mentioned
- Shown on their website profile

##### Game Week Sponsorship - £200

- Sponsor all senior games in a week of your choosing - 1st XI and 2nd XI League and Cup games, as well as Midweek XI games
- Sponsorship announcement post across all our social media
- Included in all public announcements for the game week, e.g. team announcements, results, videos, as well as plugs for your business
- Drink and tea for five guests at the Saturday home game

##### Advertising Board Sponsorship - £200/year

- 180cm x 75cm board around the ground, seen by more than 300 people every week
- Sponsorship announcement post across all our social media
- Board design approved by you
- Reduced rates for longer term sponsorship
- Initial setup cost of £120

##### Junior Section Sponsorship - £1500

- Logo included on all junior kit
- Sponsorship announcement post across all our social media
- Included in all public announcements for the season, e.g. team announcements, results, videos, as well as plugs for your business
- Invitation for you and guests to junior presentation evening

##### Club Sponsorship - negotiable

- Logo included on all playing kit for juniors and seniors
- Sponsorship announcement post across all our social media
- Included in all public announcements for the season, e.g. team announcements, results, videos, as well as plugs for your business
- Invitation for you and guests to junior presentation evening

All sponsors will be invited to our annual sponsors day, and have the opportunity to distribute promotional materials to club members.
