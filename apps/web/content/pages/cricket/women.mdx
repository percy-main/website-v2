Percy Main's women's team formed in 2024 and plays competitive softball cricket in the Women's Outdoor Bash League. We completed our first full season in 2025 and are ready to go again in 2026. Alongside the league, we enter friendly festivals throughout the summer — a great way for new players to try the game in a relaxed setting.

The team is open to all players aged 13 and over. No experience is needed — whether you've played before or have never picked up a bat, you'll be made welcome. We pride ourselves on a friendly, welcoming atmosphere both on and off the pitch, with regular club social events through the season.
