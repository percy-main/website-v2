Percy Main Cricket Club was formed in 1860. The club were Northumberland League Champions in 1953, 1977 & 1978 and winners of the Wrigleys Trophy Indoor 6 a side competition in 1978, 79 & 81.

We run 1st, 2nd & Midweek XI seniors teams, as well as a thriving junior section, and women's and girls' teams.
