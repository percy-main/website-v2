#### Directions

Take the coast road out of Newcastle. Go past the turn off for the A19 tyne Tunnel and take the next exit (which should be signposted Percy Main). Go up the slip Road and turn right at the top. This will take you onto Norham Road. Follow Norham Rd down to a large roundabout. Take the third exit from the Roundabout. This should be Wallsend Rd (don't take the dual carriageway which will be the last exit). Turn left down Station Rd and past Percy Main metro station. Take the third left past the Metro station, this is St John's Rd and the cricket ground is on the left, past St.John's Church in St.John's Green.

#### Our Facilities

- Two outdoor artificial nets
- Net cage for use on grass pitches
- 12 grass Wickets
- One artificial wicket
- Large traditional scorebox
- Full roll-on covers
