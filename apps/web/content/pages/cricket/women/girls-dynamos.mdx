Percy Main is running **Girls Dynamos** in 2026 - an ECB Dynamos Cricket programme designed specifically for girls aged 8 to 11. It's a fun, active introduction to cricket with a focus on games and friendships, not drills.

##### When

**Monday afternoons, 4.30pm - 5.30pm** at the club.

##### What to expect

Each session is built around playing cricket, with a short warm-up, a quick skills block, and plenty of game time using soft balls and light bats. Every child gets to bat, bowl and field in every session - no waiting on the sidelines. All abilities are welcome, including players who've never picked up a bat before, and the programme is designed to be inclusive and supportive of different learning needs.

Girls who enjoy Dynamos have a natural pathway into our [senior women's team](/cricket/women) when they reach 13, and into the wider [junior section](/cricket/juniors) alongside.
