## Celebrating Refugee Week 2025

[Refugee Week](https://refugeeweek.org.uk/) is the world’s largest arts & culture festival celebrating the contributions, creativity and resilience of refugees and people seeking sanctuary. This year it will be celebrated from 16th - 22nd June.

Each year's refugee week has a particular theme; this time it is _"Community as a superpower"_ - as a community sports club this is a message we stand behind firmly. One of our players, Akakhel Mashal, raised the idea of a cricket match between a Percy Main team and a team of refugees from the [Walking With](https://walkingwith.co.uk/) charity in Wallsend; we thought it sounded fantastic.

#### Presidents XI vs Walking Wickets

##### Sunday 22nd June 2025

Percy Main Cricket and Sports Club will be hosting a friendly T20 cricket match between...

**Percy Main President's XI, selected by Jon Roys**

**Walking Wickets, representing Walking With, selected by Akakhel Mashal**

Alongside the cricket there will be music, food, drink, and celebration of the North Tyneside community. We'd love to welcome you to the club to help make it a fantastic day.
