There are three parts to this news. 

Firstly the charity which is committed jn bringing sport to the local area of Percy Main will be changing its name to “PERCY MAIN COMMUNITY SPORTS CLUB”. 

Secondly we are looking to bring the main pavilion building back into use for a period of 3 years while we plan more long term redevelopments. As part of this plan will we will be crowd funding to help achieve this. So please watch out for where you can all help here. 

Finally, we are very pleased to be expanding the sports on offer at the site and we have partnered with the Boxing & Kickboxing Fundamentals Centre run by Mick Terrill. The former bar area will be converted into a gym area and Mick and his team will be offering the very best coaching and training from the site through 2025 and beyond. So watch out for more news. 

The charity and its commitment to amateur sport continue to grow and expand. We now have football, cricket, boxing and kickboxing on offer at the main. And we are not done there. Please help and support our mission in making sport available to all people.
