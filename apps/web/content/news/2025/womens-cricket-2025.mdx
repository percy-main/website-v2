Our women’s softball training will be back from the end of April this year!

Our women’s softball team are part of 2025 Women’s Outdoor Bash Softball League and enter into a number of festivals throughout summer.

Matches and festivals will be on **Sundays** from May onwards.
Training is on **Monday nights** and starts **28th April 6-7pm **

Please note you have to be over 12 to be part of the senior women’s training, any girls under 13 can also train as part of the juniors on a Tuesday night.

If you have any questions or would like any further information please drop us a message on socials or email [Megan.roys@percymain.org](mailto:megan.roys@percymain.org) for more details.
