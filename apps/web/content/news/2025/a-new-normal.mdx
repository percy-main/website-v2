Club members gathered to wish Jay Pillai the best of luck as he departs for a new life in Norway.

<Image src="/images/contentful/1d62e9e2987db85d85e9812c5844a070/WhatsApp_Image_2025-02-16_at_20.07.32.jpeg" alt="Jay's Leaving Do" caption="Jay's Leaving Do" />

Jay joined the club in 2022, and has played for 1st, 2nd and Academy XIs, as well as in the Midweek side. A superb fielder, stalwart batter, and occasional bowler, he has been a great clubman on and off the field. He leaves us having played 40 games, scoring 361 runs, taken 3 wickets, and pouched 6 catches.
