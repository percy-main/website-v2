Our winter cricket nets will begin on Wednesday 22nd January 2025, continuing every week for 8 total sessions. All players are welcome to attend - whether you've been at the club for years, making a long-awaited comeback, or looking to try a new sport!

Indoor pre-season practice is a great way to prepare for the season, meet new players, and build fitness.

**Where? **Hadrian Leisure Centre, Wallsend

**When? **Wednesday 22nd January, then every Wednesday

**Time? **8.30PM - 10PM

**Cost?** £5 per session
