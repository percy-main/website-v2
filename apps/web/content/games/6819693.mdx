#### Match Report

Percy Main 2nd XI welcomed Lintz 2nd XI to St Johns Green on Saturday, where they were hoping to build on encouraging performances from the previous two weeks. Lintz won the toss, elected to bat and got off to a solid start. Making good use of the short boundary at the scoreboard end, Lintz hit 69 off 18 overs before Main were able to take a wicket, with Singh Dogra clean bowling the Lintz opener with a lovely delivery.

An improvement in the field after a pep talk from skipper Knight saw Main able to restrict Lintz to 13 runs over the next 9 overs which included two wickets from Razqi, one of which being a maiden. Main were somewhat unlucky not to dismiss a few more in a good spell of bowling. However Lintz were able to up with run rate and with their middle order digging in they were able to reach 162/5 after 40 overs but not before well deserved dismissals from Thelwell and Mahmood.

Slaven partnered Knight in opening the batting for the first time in his career. Both looked comfortable until Slaven was unfortunate to mistime a shot to Mid Wicket. With Main needing to up the run rate, Knight was dismissed on 29 by a good delivery leaving Percy Main 32/2 after 16 overs. As the crowd started to build, swelled by an early return from the 1st XI, good weather and a steady flow of pints from the Amateurs bar, the PMCC wickets unfortunately started to tumble. The next 5 batsman collapsed for just six runs in the face of what was decent restrictive bowling from Lintz. Hopper and Singh Dogra threatened a partnership but Percy Main fell short at 53 all out .

A disappointing day for Main, improvement needed for the visit to fellow Division 6 strugglers Whitley Bay next weekend. If lessons can be learned from today and they can build on the encouraging performances in the opening two games, a much sought after victory is most definitely on the cards.
