#### Match Report

Percy Main welcomed Newcastle Super Kings to St Johns Green for a 40 over friendly match, drenched in glorious sunshine, and fragranced by the sweet smell of barbecue.

Both sides were looking for time in the middle, and a chance for players to prove themselves with both clubs having strong Saturday availability. Both sides gave every player a chance to both bowl and bat (some of them twice!) - ultimately PMCC snuck the win in a close game, but the real winner of the day was cricket.

A great game of cricket played in a great spirit by two teams who love our sport - what more could you want from a Sunday afternoon!
