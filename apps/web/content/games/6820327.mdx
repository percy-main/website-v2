#### Match Report

The 1st XI travelled to what will surely be one of the prettiest grounds of the season; sadly they produced what is likely to be one of the ugliest scorecards.

A fine straw-coloured wicket was presented by the home team, but on winning the toss the home skipper chose to ask Percy Main to bat first... perhaps he knew that looks can flatter to deceive. More likely he had been on play-cricket and seen our recent batting form. Either way it proved to be a wise decision, as wickets fell with disturbing regularity throughout our innings. The only bright spot a fluid 38 from 49 balls from the blade of returning left-hander Ben Jenkins.

Defending a total of 58 was never a realistic task, but Oli Robson hadn't read the script. With the first ball of Allendale's innings, a mistimed shot across the line went straight up, descending into the palms of Muhammad Mahmood to give hope to the visitors. An early insertion of spin gave immediate reward, with Nick Mason producing a pair of unplayable deliveries to pick up two wickets, one in each of his first two overs. 

Seeing the success of spin from one end, Anshad Pulayakalathil was brought into the attack, bowling well-flighted, deceptive leg spin. Some extremely threatening deliveries, inducing false shots and fear in the batters, were unjustly not rewarded with success on the scoresheet.

A tough day out, with our batting frailties exposed by a decent, if not spectacular, Allendale attack. Percy Main will be looking ahead to next week's home fixture with a Bomarsund team who were well beaten this week, and looking to condemn their guests to back-to-back defeats.
