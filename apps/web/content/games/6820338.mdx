#### Match Report

Percy Main lost the toss and were sent into the field on a warm day at St John’s Green. With a couple of early wickets the main looked right in it with Bomarsund reduced to 46-2. However, a third wicket stand of 145, alongside some lacklustre fielding, turned the match firmly in Bomarsund’s favour. Two more late wickets from Ollie Robson (3-37) saw Bomarsund finish on 215-5 with their opener not out on 135. Have to say despite a couple tough chances it was a brilliant knock, well batted.

This left an uphill task in the second half. With a few twenty’s but no major scores of note. The main finished on 147 all out with Skipper Al Young top scoring on 23.

Another loss for the lads but we go again at Greenside away next Saturday. UP THE MAIN!
