#### Match Report

The 1st XI fell to their second defeat of the season to Berwick CC, but didn't go down without a fight.

Winning the toss, home captain Al decided to put the visitors in to bat - April cricket, a strong bowling lineup, and a poor batting performance from the visitors all supported that decision. Reality, sadly, did not.

Three hours and fifty overs later, the lads were sat eating their tea having taken 10 wickets. Berwick's Aussie superstar's tea likely tasted better, accompanied as it was by the side dish of a run-a-ball chanceless ton. Nonetheless, returning bowlers Sam Byerley and Nick Dodds had strong showings, and deserved the success they found.

An imposing total of 256 was always going to be a challenge to chase, and a couple of early wickets put the wind in Berwick's sails. Akakel Mashal took this as personal affront, and wrought havoc upon the visitors' economy rates in a 32 ball score of 61, including seven sixes. The pick of the shots was a lofted extra cover drive that sailed well over the top of the clubhouse.

The Main middle order faltered, and it wasn't until Nick Dodds came in at 8, biffing an aggressive run-a-ball 51 not out, that some hope was restored. Further resistance came from number 11 Anshad Pulayakalathil, his 22 runs coming at a strike rate of 122, and ensuring an extra couple of batting points for the home side.

Next week we travel to Allendale, where we'll be looking to nail down that first W of the year.
