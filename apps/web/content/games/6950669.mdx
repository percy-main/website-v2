#### Match Report

The game was at Belsay cricket club on a cold, rainy and windy day. Typical early season weather that made bowling difficult as the weather got worse with rain and blustery wind. 

Main started the game well with Finn taking a wicket with the first ball of the game.  Henry replaced him bowling downhill but the wind was making bowling difficult through the game. Harrison bowled uphill and was unlucky when a difficult catch went down but the lads kept working hard in the field. Later in the innings Jack took a great catch despite an earlier injury from Harrison’s bowling.  Woody bowled very well and was the only bowler not to concede a wide. The bowling highlight was Finn taking an athletic caught and bowled, finishing the 4 wickets for 8 runs. South North made 116 and unfortunately we conceeded 42 wides.

The battling started well with Henry playing well hitting 3 fours before being out caught and bowled for 18. Finn played well making 11 before also being out caught and bowled. We lost a few wickets and then good batting from Harrison, Max and Dan got us into a good position in the later stages of the game. In the last over we needed 10 to win and we just fell short ending on 107/7 from 20 overs.

Well done to everyone and there’s lots of positives to work on in training before the next match.
