#### Match Report

Percy Main welcomed newly promoted Hexham Leazes for the first game of 2025.

Arriving at St Johns Green, immaculate ground drenched in sunshine, caffeine from a large energy drink coursing through the veins, and terrifying faux confidence of a strong winter net regime spinning through heads; one could be forgiven for daring to dream that this might be the year we would see off-field promise converted into on-field success.

Alas, skipper Al's first action of the day was to lose the toss. Hexham chose to bowl, and started strongly with accurate bowling from their opening tandem getting the most from an occasionally tricky surface. A steady stream of wickets fell throughout the innings, and scoring runs proved challenging. Channelling his inner Boycott, Al top scored with 24 not out from 88 deliveries. Fresh signing John Allan hit a fluent 16, with Akakhel Mashal contributing an aggressively struck 14. Batter of the day would go to Percy Main number 11 Oli Robson, perhaps the only player from either team to make scoring runs look simple as he progressed to 12 from 15 balls, before being captured by a fine nick behind.

So it transpired that Hexham Leazes had 50 overs to chase 85 runs. Their opening pair walked out full of confidence, but the gravity of their task must surely have become clear when they immediately encountered the accuracy and guile of PMCC opening bowler Oli Robson. His first victim lasted just three balls before the edge was extracted, and happily pouched at first slip.

All-rounder John started well, applying good pressure from the other end, and Oli quickly removed the other opening bat, before John sent the Leazes number 4 and 5 back to the pavilion in rapid succession. At 22-4 Hexham were on the ropes, and the lads from the main, perhaps inspired by the new boxing gym in the clubhouse, were looking for a knockout blow.

Unfortunately the away team caught a second wind, guided by their all-rounder Rob Hook playing a cautious and considered marathon innings, ending with 26 from 75 balls. Brief bursts of optimism punctuated for Percy Main, with Mashal, Anshad, and Waqas picking up a wicket each. Ultimately Hexham Leazes stuck to their task, got in behind it, and ground their way to victory 7 wickets down.

All told, a competitive game, with many positives to take from it. The effort and concentration in the field was there throughout, and forced Hexham to work hard for their win - this attitude _will_ bring rewards if maintained all year.

Next up, the 1st XI welcome Berwick to St Johns Green on the 26th April.
