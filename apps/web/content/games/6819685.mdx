#### Match Report

PMCC 2nd team were away at Stocksfield playing their 3rd team. Skipper S Knight won the toss and batted first. Batsmen all had the words of the skipper “Value your wicket” in their heads. On a typical up and down April wicket PMCC got to 70-2 with a steady 50 partnership between B Duncan Jr and B Hopper. PMCC ended up on 136 - 6 with Craig Dixon top scoring with 37.

Clearly Stocksfield fancied winning before NUFC played at 5-30. However, good bowling from Aaditya Kapil and Harihara Subramanian with 3 wickets each and strong aggressive fielding and catching had Stocksfield at 84 - 7. PMCC bowling attack while good at times lacked consistency and despite the skipper ringing the changes and setting traps, in the 37th over Stocksfield reached the target for 7 wickets down.

The first game of the season often sets the standards for the season and thankfully PMCC valued their wickets and fielded well, the consistent bowling will come next week.
