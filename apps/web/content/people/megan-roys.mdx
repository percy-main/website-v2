Megan has been heading our women’s section since its inception in 2024. 

She has, however, been a part of the wider club for a lot longer, with her dad, brother, grandad and great grandad having been members also. 

Megan hopes to continue the growth and strength of the women’s team this year and is looking forward to playing in the softball league. 

Outside of cricket, she also plays netball as well as being a keen crossfitter, and helping to running PMCCs social media - we aren’t sure how she has time to fit it all in!
