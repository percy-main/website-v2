James Dance is an experienced cricketer who has spent the past 15 years playing league cricket in Durham and the North East Cricket League. Over the course of his career he has represented several clubs, including Marsden Cricket Club, Boldon CA, and Whiteleas Cricket Club, building up many years of first-team experience.

Having recently moved north of the water, James joined Percy Main Cricket Club for the 2026 season. He brings a wealth of experience to the side and continues to enjoy the game in the right spirit - playing for the enjoyment of cricket while still maintaining a strong competitive edge.
